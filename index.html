About KUAC

A Practical Response to Displacement

Krains UniAid Center helps displaced families navigate travel, arrival, and settlement across Europe with less confusion and more clarity. We listen first, explain options in plain language, and connect people to the right help quickly.

The goal is to help people arrive safely, stay informed, and get support that makes the next stage of life more stable.

What KUAC covers

  • Travel planning, route guidance, and document preparation
  • Arrival help, shelter referrals, and emergency contacts
  • Food, healthcare, and essentials coordination through partners
  • Family support, school access, and settlement referrals

How KUAC Supports People

KUAC is a humanitarian support hub focused on the EU region. We help people move safely, reach trusted local services, and take the next step toward housing, schooling, work, and stability with dignity.

The work is simple and direct: reduce confusion, protect dignity, and connect each person to the right help as quickly as possible. That can include route planning, emergency contacts, shelter referrals, local language support, and follow-up after arrival.

Travel Support

Route planning, transit guidance, document checklists, and practical travel preparation for safe movement across borders.

Arrival Assistance

Temporary shelter referrals, local contacts, emergency guidance, and first steps toward housing, food, and healthcare access.

Stability Path

Longer-term support, including education referrals, job search preparation, and reliable community resources that help families rebuild.
